# Initial Concept
This repository compiles a collection of fundamental market making models and explorations.

# Product Guide: LOB Modeling

## Target Audience
The primary audience for this repository includes **Trading Desks and Firms** looking to evaluate and implement market making models. This includes quantitative researchers, developers, and analysts interested in Limit Order Book (LOB) dynamics.

## Product Vision
To provide a robust, well-documented, and extensible library of market making models that can be used for research, evaluation, and as a foundation for developing proprietary trading strategies.

## Core Goals
*   **Documentation & Usability**: Enhance the project's documentation and usability to make it easier for new users to understand and use the models.
*   **Model Portfolio Expansion**: Continuously expand the collection of LOB models to include a wider range of market conditions and trading scenarios.
*   **Refactoring & Cleanup**: Periodically refactor and clean up the codebase to improve its quality, maintainability, and performance.

## Key Features
*   **Diverse Model Library**: A wide selection of foundational and advanced market making models (e.g., Kyle, Almgren-Chriss, Glosten-Milgrom, etc.).
*   **Example Notebooks**: Comprehensive Jupyter notebooks providing step-by-step demonstrations and visualizations of each model.
*   **Command Line Interface**: Easy-to-use CLI commands for running models and tests.
*   **Python API**: A clean and intuitive Python API for integrating models into other projects and workflows.

## Quality Standards
*   **Refactored Logic**: Ensure that the core model logic is clear, efficient, and well-organized.
*   **Comprehensive Documentation**: Maintain high-quality docstrings and usage examples for all models and utilities.
*   **Extensibility**: Design the library to be easily extended with new models and features.

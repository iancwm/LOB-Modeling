# Product Guidelines: LOB Modeling

## Documentation & Prose Style
*   **Formal & Academic**: Use precise, mathematically-rigorous language to describe models and their underlying theories. Ensure all symbols and parameters are clearly defined.
*   **Mathematical Accuracy**: Prioritize accuracy in formulas and derivations over simplified explanations.

## Tone and Voice
*   **Pragmatic & Solution-Oriented**: Maintain a professional and helpful tone, focused on providing practical implementations of market making models.
*   **Clear & Direct**: Use simple, direct language when providing instructions or explanations, while still adhering to a formal tone.

## Naming Conventions
*   **Domain-Specific Naming**: Use names that are consistent with the terminology used in quantitative finance and LOB modeling (e.g., `alpha` for market impact, `kappa` for mean-reversion speed).
*   **Consistent Variable Names**: Ensure that variables used in code and documentation are consistent across different models and examples.

## UX & Interaction Principles
*   **Simplicity & Focus**: Keep the focus on the models themselves, avoiding unnecessary complexity in the code or notebooks.
*   **Clear Visualizations**: Use high-quality, informative plots and charts to illustrate model behavior and performance.
*   **Consistent Formatting**: Maintain a consistent layout and style for all Jupyter notebooks to ensure they are easy to read and understand.
